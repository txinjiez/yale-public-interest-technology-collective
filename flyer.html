Our Mission

Public Interest Technology Student Collective (PITSCY) is the only student organization at Yale dedicated to tech for social impact. PITSCY empowers students to understand, critique, and shape technologies that impact society.
We connect students with faculty, practitioners, and hands-on experiences to build a community of responsible, civic-minded technologists.

Our Three Pillars
Activation
Exploring public interest technology through workshops and speaker series
Community
Building a cross-disciplinary network across CS, law, policy, and the humanities
Impact
Working on real-world projects with nonprofits, government, and civic organizations
Programming
Speaker Events
Hosting technologists, policymakers, and advocates to share pathways into public interest tech
Pro-Bono Projects
Connecting students with opportunities to develop software for New Haven nonprofits and city agencies
Career-Building
Engaging students in hackathons, policy labs, career fairs, applied workshops, case study research
