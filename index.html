Our Mission

Building a more equitable technological future

The Public Interest Technology Student Collective exists to empower Yale students to understand, critique, and shape technologies that impact society. We serve students across disciplines who seek to design, govern, and use technology in ways that advance equity, accountability, and the public good. We are building a community of practice—connecting students with faculty, practitioners, and hands-on learning and professional experiences that cultivate responsible, civic-minded technologists.

Education

Workshops, speaker series, and coursework to develop the next generation of public interest technologists.

Community

A vibrant network connecting students across law, CS, public health, policy, and the humanities.

Impact

Real-world projects with nonprofits, government agencies, and civic organizations across Connecticut and beyond.

Community

Speaker Events & Career Exploration

Through our ongoing speaker series, we bring together technologists, policymakers, lawyers, and advocates who have built careers at the intersection of technology and the public good. Each event is designed not just to inform, but to open doors — giving Yale students a window into what it actually looks like to work in public interest tech, government, nonprofits, and beyond.
